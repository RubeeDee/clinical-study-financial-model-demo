# Clinical Study Financial Feasibility Model

## Project Overview

This is an Excel financial model I built to estimate clinical study costs and assess whether projected drug purchases could cover the cost of the study.

The model compares different patient recruitment strategies, drug prices, and patient attrition scenarios over a 12-month period.

## What the Model Does

- Estimates research costs using different sampling strategies
- Calculates expected drug purchases
- Accounts for patient attrition
- Compares different drug price scenarios
- Projects purchases over a 12-month period
- Calculates the net financial result
- Classifies each scenario as profit, loss, or breakeven

## Excel Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel
- VLOOKUP
- IF functions
- SUM
- Linked worksheets
- Lookup tables
- Financial modelling
- Scenario analysis
- Data validation

## Model Structure

### Estimated Budget

Contains the estimated costs for different study recruitment strategies and a detailed breakdown of research expenses.

### Summary Data

Uses the assumptions from the lookup table to calculate drug purchases, research costs, net results, and financial outcomes.

## Key Question

The model answers a simple business question:

**Under different study and pricing scenarios, can projected drug purchases cover the estimated cost of the research?**

## Important Note

This is a fictional demonstration project created for portfolio purposes.

All figures, assumptions, patient numbers, drug prices, and costs are illustrative and do not represent any confidential clinical study, company budget, or proprietary information.

## Key Insights

The model shows how changes in patient recruitment, attrition, drug pricing, and study costs can affect the financial outcome of a clinical study.

It can be used to compare different scenarios and identify conditions where projected drug purchases may or may not cover research costs.
